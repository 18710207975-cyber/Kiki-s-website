02 / DIGITAL PRACTICE

Weekly Reflection.

01 💚

Week 1

The first week is introduction week, it was the very first time that I got to know about digital media and digital practice. Professor Holly Steel, who is a very cute and kind teacher, intreduced us to the course and the content of the course. I found that we all live in the digital world,and digital media is everywhere in our daily life. I learned that digital media is a new way to express ourselves and communicate with others.

02 🧡

Week 2

In the second week, we finally started to explore the real-world of digital practice. We had our first workshop, and we learned how to use different tools such as VS Code, GitHub and even our school's own website to create our own website. I learned a little bit about HTML and CSS, and I found that it is not as difficult as I thought. I found that I can use these tools to create my own website and express myself in a digital way. Even though I'm a beginner, I can't make every code perfect, but I can learn from my mistakes and improve myself. I think digital practice is a very interesting and useful skill, and I ant to learn more about it in the future.
